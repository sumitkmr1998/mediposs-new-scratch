## Version 2.0.4 Changes

**Features & Improvements:**
* **Stock Management:** Added support to quickly add new stock to an existing medicine batch without re-entering the batch number.
* **Security & Permissions:** Restructured permissions so staff can only add stock. Direct edits to total quantities now require explicit override permissions.
* **Multi-Location Support:** Upgraded bulk purchase and stock entry to allow manual quantity distribution across multiple locations.
* **UI/UX Optimizations (Warehouse):** 
  * Swapped layout globally to consistently display the **Store on the left** and **Clinic on the right**.
  * Restored the two-row layout (bulk operations at the top, store/dispense at the bottom).
  * Consolidated the search area into a unified search bar (matching the bulk transfer panel) and compacted text sizes for batch numbers to reduce clutter.
